Information We Collect

We collect minimal data to improve your experience:

  • Usage Statistics: Anonymous data about app downloads and page views
  • User Preferences: Theme preferences, wishlist, and profile settings stored locally
  • Reviews & Ratings: Content you voluntarily submit about apps
  • Download History: List of apps you've downloaded (stored in your browser)

How We Use Your Data

  • To personalize your experience on Stardust Store
  • To improve app recommendations and rankings
  • To provide real-time updates on app downloads and ratings
  • To moderate reviews and maintain platform quality

Cookies & Local Storage

We use localStorage and sessionStorage for essential functionality only:

  • Remember your theme preference (dark/light mode)
  • Store your wishlist items
  • Save your download history
  • No third-party tracking cookies are used

Third-Party Services

We use Firebase (by Google) for real-time database services. Firebase is GDPR-compliant and follows strict security protocols. Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

Your Rights

  • Access: You can view all your data in your profile
  • Delete: Clear your local data anytime from browser settings
  • Opt-out: You can stop using the store at any time

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including HTTPS encryption, secure Firebase authentication, and regular security audits. Your data is never sold or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
